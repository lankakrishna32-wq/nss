Our Scheme

Hamara Bharat

Hamara Bharat is a non-profit, service-oriented organization initiated by National Service Scheme (NSS) alumni, volunteers and coordinators who share a common commitment to serving society and contributing to the nation. Our journey began through NSS, where we experienced the true meaning of service, responsibility, teamwork and community engagement. Inspired by these values and guided by our respected NSS faculty mentor, we have come together to continue that journey beyond our college years. We believe that service has no age, boundary or deadline. The spirit of volunteering that begins during student life can become a lifelong commitment to society.

MOTTO: "United by Service. Driven by Purpose." Purpose: Education through Service
Our Vision

A Bharat where every person has the opportunity to live with dignity, every community has the strength to shape its own future, and every citizen finds a meaningful way to serve.

Our Mission

To build a culture of lifelong service by bringing people together, understanding real community needs, developing capable volunteers, empowering communities, connecting resources and creating sustainable solutions that strengthen society.
